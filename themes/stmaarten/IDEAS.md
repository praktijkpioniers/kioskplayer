# St Maarten (11 November)

Kids with lanterns singing door to door; St Martin shares his cloak with a beggar.
Code: `themes/stmaarten.lua` (installed from engine.lua); art: `tools/bake/{kids,lanterns,martin}.py`.
The table of ideas and their status is `ideas.csv`.

Preview keys: **F5** St Martin + a beggar (the cloak act), **F6** kids (meetings certain for 90 s),
**F7** a procession, **F8** a beggar.

## The cloak act
St Martin (Roman soldier, red cloak) finds a beggar, draws his sword, cuts the cloak and hands the
piece over; the beggar wraps it round, stands up and walks off. Four stages of cloak. With nothing
left he sits down in the cold: now he is a beggar, and a new St Martin comes along for him.
One Martin per room.

## The moon
The moon hangs top left for a few minutes. Then it sinks, warming to lantern colour, onto the bare
stick of a child who lost their lantern, or it flies loose; if it misses, the child chases it to the
next screen. When the sky has had no moon long enough, a lit lantern slips off a child's stick and
floats up, wobbling, to become the new moon (the child is left with a bare stick).
Preview: **Ctrl+F5** the moon comes down, **Ctrl+F6** a new moon goes up.

## The church
A village church stands a while (behind the walkers). Now and then the bell rings and the door opens:
the beggars sitting about get up and go in, out of the cold. Then somebody comes out: St Martin (most
likely when the room has none), a beggar, a child with a lantern, or the rich man. Sometimes a child
fetches a beggar first, holds out a hand, and they go in together; the child comes out again.
Preview: **Shift+F8** the bell and the service, **Ctrl+F8** a child takes a beggar to church.

## Lantern trouble
Now and then a candle sets a paper lantern on fire: the child stops and watches it burn; ash falls,
a bare smoking stick is left, and the nearest friend brings a new lantern. A child with an LED lantern
(on a black battery stick) sees it die, shakes it, and it flickers back on (or stays dead).
Preview: **Ctrl+F7** a lantern catches fire, **Alt+F7** the LED lantern dies.

## Meetings
Kids sing for St Martin (sweets for everyone) or for a beggar (one child walks up with a gift:
a sweet, a mandarin, an apple, a present, or their own lantern left glowing by the beggar).
Groups join each other's singing.

## The side cast (stm_extra.lua, theme.extra.json)
Houses on the doorbell round (a grumpy one turns the light off), a cow and a bonfire to sing at,
geese that honk St Martin out of hiding, two teenagers without lanterns (a turnip), the Dom of
Utrecht. Sint Maarten the island: Columbus bumps into it on 11-11-1493, the Frenchman and the
Dutchman squabble and St Martin cuts it in two like his cloak; the border walk (the Dutchman
naps, maybe under a piece of cloak); a jet over Maho beach. At 11:11 the carnival parade crosses
the room once. Keys: F9 (Shift: grumpy), Ctrl+F9, F10, F11, F12, Shift+F10/F11/F12, Ctrl+F10.
